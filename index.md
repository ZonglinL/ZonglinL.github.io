## About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Central Florida, advised by [Chen Chen](https://www.crcv.ucf.edu/chenchen/), and a Student Researcher at ByteDance Seed. My research focuses on computer vision, generative models, and multimodal learning, with interests in visual representation learning and controllable image and video generation.

Previously, I worked with [Chen Feng](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=YeG8ZM0AAAAJ&hl=en) at [AI4CE Lab](https://ai4ce.github.io/), New York University.

## Education

- **Jan 2025 – Present:** Ph.D. in Computer Science, University of Central Florida
- **Aug 2024 – Dec 2024:** Ph.D. in Computer Science, University of Utah (transferred)
- **Sep 2021 – Dec 2022:** M.S. in Operations Research, Columbia University
- **Sep 2017 – Jun 2020:** B.S. in Applied Mathematics, University of California, San Diego
